# Page Spec — Services `/services`

## Job of the page
Explain breadth without turning the site into a long list of generic capabilities.

## Suggested service groups
1. Creative Strategy
2. Social Campaigns
3. Content Production
4. Brand & Design
5. Film / Motion / Reels
6. Creator & Influencer
7. AI Creative
8. Performance Creative Adaptation

## Sections
1. Header
2. Services Hero
3. Service Chapters / Accordion
4. Deliverables Matrix
5. Process Timeline
6. Related Work
7. Final CTA
8. Footer

## Per-service information model
- one sentence: what it solves;
- typical outputs;
- where it works in the funnel;
- one relevant project thumbnail;
- CTA to brief.

## Avoid
Do not repeat "What is X?" textbook definitions. Assume the client understands the service category; explain 3seconds' approach and value.

## Definition of done
- Layout follows the relevant section specs in `03-sections/`.
- Works with keyboard and touch.
- Reduced-motion state is intentional.
- No layout shift from media loading.
- Route has unique title/description/OG metadata.
- Mobile composition is reviewed separately rather than auto-stacked blindly.
