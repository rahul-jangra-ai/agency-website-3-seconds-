# Home Section — 3-Second Hero

## Message
Recommended headline: **Make the first 3 seconds count.**
Support: `Creative strategy, campaigns and content built to stop the scroll.`

## Layout — desktop
- Full viewport minimum height.
- Huge headline split across 2–3 lines.
- Showreel/media occupies 35–50% of screen and may partially sit behind type.
- Bottom edge holds two CTAs: `See the work` and `Start a brief`.
- Tiny timecode/recording motif adds the brand signature.

## Motion
- Main copy is visible immediately.
- During first ~900ms, a subtle `03 → 02 → 01` overlay/tick sequence passes through without blocking content.
- Showreel begins muted after poster is visible.
- Headline may shift 1–2vw with scroll for depth, but remains readable.

## Mobile
Use a portrait media crop under headline. Keep headline within ~55–65% of first viewport so CTAs remain visible quickly.

## Acceptance criteria
- LCP should be a poster/image/text, not a delayed video frame.
- Hero communicates agency category instantly.
- Video failure still yields a complete hero.
