# Home Section — Manifesto

## Purpose
Give 3seconds a distinct belief system.

## Suggested copy structure
Large statement:
**Attention isn't a metric after the work. It's the material we work with.**

Three short supporting ideas:
- Hook fast.
- Reward attention.
- Leave a memory.

## Interaction
Sticky left-side headline while the three ideas rotate or snap in on the right. Keep the entire sequence under ~2.5 viewport heights.
