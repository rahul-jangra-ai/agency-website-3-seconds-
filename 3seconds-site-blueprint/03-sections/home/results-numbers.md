# Home Section — Results / Numbers

## Purpose
Quantified proof.

## Examples of metric types
- campaign views;
- watch time;
- engagement lift;
- assets shipped;
- launch turnaround;
- creator reach.

## Visual
2–4 giant metrics with one-line context. One metric can fill the viewport.

## Integrity
Only publish verifiable numbers. Include the unit and context; avoid vanity numbers without explanation.
