# Voice & Copy Tone

## Personality
Fast. Sharp. Visual. Culturally literate. Never try-hard.

## Writing rules
- Prefer 3–8 word headlines.
- Avoid generic agency phrases: "360-degree solutions", "synergy", "innovative solutions", "we are passionate".
- Use active verbs: Make, Cut, Launch, Shoot, Shift, Build, Move.
- Claims must be provable.
- Humor should be occasional, not constant.

## Example positioning directions
- **Make the first 3 seconds count.**
- **Stop the scroll. Start something.**
- **Ideas built for attention.**
- **Creative that earns the next second.**

## CTA language
Primary options:
- Start a brief
- Make something
- Talk to us

Secondary options:
- See the work
- View case study
- Explore services

## Microcopy motif
Timecode can frame small labels:
- `00:00:01 — Hook`
- `00:00:02 — Hold`
- `00:00:03 — Hit`

Do not overuse this on every section.
