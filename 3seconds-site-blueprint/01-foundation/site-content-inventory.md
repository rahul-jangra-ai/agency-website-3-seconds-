# Site Content Inventory

## Brand essentials required before build lock
- Final wordmark / logo for **3seconds**
- One-sentence positioning statement
- Primary CTA label
- Contact email
- Social handles
- Location(s), if public

## Portfolio assets
For each launch case study, collect:
- project name;
- client name;
- year;
- category / service tags;
- 1 hero film or loop;
- 4–12 stills / frames;
- challenge;
- insight;
- creative idea;
- deliverables;
- metrics / outcomes;
- testimonial if available;
- credits.

Recommended launch minimum: **6 projects**, with **3 fully developed case studies**.

## Service taxonomy
Recommended core service groups:
1. Creative Strategy
2. Social & Campaigns
3. Content Production
4. Brand & Design
5. Motion / Film / Reels
6. Creator & Influencer
7. AI Creative / Experiments
8. Media Adaptation / Performance Creative

## Proof assets
- client logos;
- campaign metrics;
- awards only if legitimate;
- testimonial quotes;
- press mentions only if legitimate.

## People/culture assets
- team portraits or candid production images;
- behind-the-scenes footage;
- office/studio details;
- short staff quotes;
- job openings.
